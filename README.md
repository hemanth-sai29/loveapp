# Know Her ❤️
> **“A little digital book about the girl I love.”**

A romantic, elegant, modern relationship questionnaire and personalized user manual web application designed to help you learn, cherish, and remember the little things about your partner.

---

## 🌸 Key Features

* **Romantic & Elegant Aesthetics**: Editorial typography (Cormorant Garamond, Playfair Display & Plus Jakarta Sans), soft rose and blush palettes, glassmorphism, and gentle floating animations.
* **10-Step Interactive Questionnaire**:
  1. **A Little About You 🌸**: Basic details, pet names, favorites, and instant happy triggers.
  2. **Favorite Foods 😋**: Ultimate favorites, comfort food, snacks, drinks, desserts, and dietary/allergy safety notes.
  3. **Daily Habits 🌷**: Chronotypes (*Morning Person / Night Owl*), stress relief, what calms her, communication preferences.
  4. **Love & Relationship ❤️**: Primary love languages, support preferences when upset, ideal date, and relationship vows.
  5. **Important Dates 🗓️**: Categorized timeline of birthdays, anniversaries, family milestones, and personal events (up to 50 dates).
  6. **Likes & Dislikes 💗**: Things she loves (movies, books, music, games) vs. things to avoid (pet peeves, dislikes, discomforts).
  7. **Her Dreams 🌙**: Starry-themed aspiration canvas for travels, career goals, and shared adventures.
  8. **A Little About Us 💕**: First impressions, favorite memories together, and sweet promises.
  9. **Rapid-Fire Round ⚡**: Playful binary instinct choices (*Coffee vs. Tea, Sunrise vs. Sunset, Beach vs. Mountains*, etc.).
  10. **Review Answers**: Section-by-section review with direct edit shortcuts that preserve all other answers.
* **Emotional Final Message 💌**: Celebratory confetti, romantic vows, and safe storage confirmation.
* **Her User Manual — Handle With Love ❤️**:
  * Personalized dashboard dynamically built from her answers.
  * **Things He Should Remember ❤️**: A dedicated boyfriend cheat sheet with her favorite food, chocolate, birthday, upcoming dates, how to support her when sad, and things to avoid.
* **Device-Local Privacy**: All data is stored in the browser's `localStorage` (`girlfriend_profile_v1`). No servers or analytics receive your personal data.
* **Autosave & Welcome Back**: Changes are saved automatically in real-time. Returning to the app opens a *“Welcome back ❤️ Continue where you left off?”* restoration dialog.
* **Keepsake Export**:
  * **Export JSON**: Download structured JSON file anytime.
  * **Print / Save as PDF**: Formatted keepsake print layout.
* **Preview Demo Data**: Includes a realistic sample profile to explore the full dashboard in one click, with a reset option.
